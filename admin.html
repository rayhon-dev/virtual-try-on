Garment Image Format Guide

For optimal virtual try-on drape and alignment, follow these criteria:

  • View: Direct FRONT view, flat-lay or ghost mannequin.
  • Subject: Garment only (no person, model, or hanger visible).
  • Sleeves: Hanging straight down close to body (10–15° angle, not spread wide).
  • Symmetry: Left and right sides reasonably symmetric.
  • Margin: Whole garment visible with a small border margin.
  • Background: Transparent PNG (best) or uniform white/light grey.
  • Resolution: High resolution (min 800px, ideally 1000px+).
  • Lighting: Flat, even lighting with no harsh background cast shadows.
Avoid: Sleeves spread like wings (causes cape-like distortion), folded hems, side angles, or model wearers.
